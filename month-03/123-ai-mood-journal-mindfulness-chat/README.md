# AI Mood Journal & Mindfulness Chat 🚀
> A web-based chatbot that guides users through daily mood check-ins, offers mindfulness prompts, and provides a private space for journaling

## Description
This web-based chatbot serves as a supportive digital companion designed to enhance mental well-being. It guides users through daily mood check-ins, offering a structured approach to self-reflection and emotional awareness. Beyond just tracking, the platform delivers personalized mindfulness prompts and maintains a secure, private space for users to journal their thoughts and feelings confidentially.

## Features
*   **Interactive Chatbot Interface**: Engage with an AI-powered conversational agent for guided interactions.
*   **Daily Mood Check-ins**: Log your current mood and reflect on your emotional state with structured prompts.
*   **Personalized Mindfulness Prompts**: Receive tailored exercises and suggestions to foster relaxation and presence.
*   **Private Digital Journal**: A secure space to record thoughts, feelings, and experiences confidentially.
*   **Mood Trend Tracking**: Visualize your emotional patterns over time through consistent logging.
*   **User-Friendly Web Interface**: Accessible and intuitive design for a seamless user experience.
*   **No Setup Required**: Simply open in a browser to start your wellness journey.

## Tech Stack
*   HTML5
*   CSS3
*   JavaScript (for chatbot logic, AI/NLP integration, and dynamic content)

## Key Concepts Demonstrated
*   **AI**: Powers the intelligent conversational flow of the chatbot, adapting to user input and providing relevant responses.
*   **NLP (Natural Language Processing)**: Used to understand and interpret user's natural language input during mood check-ins and journal entries.
*   **Chatbot**: Provides the primary interactive interface, acting as a conversational guide for users through their wellness journey.
*   **Mental Wellness**: The core objective of the project, offering tools and support for improving emotional health and self-awareness.
*   **Mood Tracking**: Enables users to consistently monitor and reflect on their emotional states over time, fostering greater insight.

## Getting Started
- Open `index.html` in your browser
