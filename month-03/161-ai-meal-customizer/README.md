## Description
The AI Meal Customizer is an innovative web application that empowers users to effortlessly adapt existing recipes to meet specific dietary needs. By leveraging artificial intelligence, this tool intelligently analyzes ingredients and cooking methods to suggest appropriate modifications, making healthy and inclusive eating more accessible. It's designed to transform any recipe into a personalized culinary experience, accommodating a range of dietary preferences and restrictions.

## Features
*   **Recipe Input**: Users can easily input any existing recipe, either by typing or pasting.
*   **Dietary Restriction Selection**: Choose from a variety of dietary options like vegan, gluten-free, low-carb, and more.
*   **AI-Powered Modification**: An intelligent AI engine processes the recipe to suggest relevant and practical modifications.
*   **Ingredient Substitution**: Automatically identifies and proposes suitable alternative ingredients based on chosen restrictions.
*   **Modified Recipe Output**: Presents a clear, revised version of the recipe, highlighting changes.
*   **User-Friendly Interface**: An intuitive and clean design ensures a seamless user experience.
*   **Supports Multiple Restrictions**: Capable of handling various dietary requirements simultaneously or individually.

## Key Concepts Demonstrated
*   **AI**: Utilized for intelligent analysis, understanding, and modification of recipe data based on complex rules and preferences.
*   **Recipe Modification**: Core functionality revolves around programmatically altering ingredients, quantities, and instructions to fit new criteria.
*   **Dietary Needs**: Directly addresses user requirements for specific diets (e.g., vegan, gluten-free, low-carb) by adapting recipes accordingly.
