# AI Plant Doctor 🚀
> Users upload a photo of their plant, and the AI analyzes the image to identify potential diseases or pest infestations. The application provides instant diagnoses and actionable recommendations for plant care.

## Description
The AI Plant Doctor is an innovative web application designed to empower plant enthusiasts and gardeners by providing instant, AI-driven diagnoses for plant ailments. Users can easily upload a photo of their ailing plant, and our sophisticated AI model, leveraging advanced computer vision techniques, analyzes the image to accurately identify potential diseases or pest infestations, offering timely insights and actionable advice for treatment. This project aims to simplify plant care, enabling early detection and effective management of plant health issues.

## Features
*   **Intuitive Image Upload:** Seamlessly upload plant photos directly from your device.
