# AI Reflective Journal 🚀
> A web application where users can log daily journal entries. The AI analyzes the text for sentiment, identifies recurring themes, and visualizes emotional trends over time, offering prompts and personalized insights for self-reflection and well-being.

## Description
The AI Reflective Journal is a powerful web application designed to enhance self-awareness through daily journaling. It leverages artificial intelligence to analyze user entries, providing objective feedback on emotional states and identifying persistent thought patterns. This tool aims to support mental well-being by transforming raw journal data into actionable insights for personal growth.

## Features
*   **Daily Journal Logging**: A straightforward interface for users to record their thoughts and experiences each day.
*   **AI-Powered Sentiment Analysis**: Automatically processes journal entries to detect and categorize emotional tone (e.g., positive, negative, neutral).
*   **Recurring Theme Identification**: AI algorithms pinpoint and highlight common topics, keywords, and patterns across entries over time.
*   **Emotional Trend Visualization**: Presents clear, interactive charts and graphs to visualize changes in sentiment and emotional states over various periods.
*   **Personalized Insights & Prompts**: Generates unique reflections and thought-provoking questions based on the analysis of journal content.
*   **Self-Reflection & Well-being Support**: Designed to help users track their emotional journey, understand their inner world, and foster overall mental wellness.

## Tech Stack
*   HTML5
*   CSS3

## Key Concepts Demonstrated
*   **AI**: Powers the analytical engine for sentiment analysis, theme identification, and generation of personalized insights.
*   **NLP (Natural Language Processing)**: Utilized to parse, understand, and extract meaningful data from raw journal text.
*   **Sentiment Analysis**: A core NLP technique applied to evaluate the emotional content and tone of user entries.
*   **Journaling**: Provides the foundational interface and purpose for users to log and track their daily thoughts and experiences.
*   **Data Trends**: Visualizes the evolution of sentiments and themes over time, allowing users to observe patterns in their emotional journey.
*   **Personal Insights**: Generates unique, reflective feedback and prompts specifically tailored to the user's individual entries and patterns.
