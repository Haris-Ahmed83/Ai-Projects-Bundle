# AI Recipe Health Optimizer 🚀
> Users can input a recipe, and the AI will analyze its nutritional content. It then suggests healthier ingredient substitutions or preparation methods to optimize the recipe for improved health and specific dietary needs.

## Description
The AI Recipe Health Optimizer is an innovative web application designed to empower users to make healthier dietary choices effortlessly. By leveraging artificial intelligence, it meticulously analyzes the nutritional profile of any user-submitted recipe. The system then intelligently suggests ingredient substitutions or alternative preparation methods to enhance the recipe's health benefits, catering to a range of dietary goals.

## Features
*   **Intuitive Recipe Input:** Easily submit recipes via text or structured input fields for analysis.
*   **Comprehensive Nutritional Breakdown:** Receive detailed analysis of calories, macronutrients (protein, fat, carbs), vitamins, and minerals.
*   **AI-Driven Health Optimization:** Get intelligent suggestions for healthier ingredient substitutions (e.g., lean protein, whole grains, healthier fats).
*   **Smart Preparation Method Advice:** Discover alternative cooking techniques to reduce unhealthy elements like excessive fat or sugar, or to preserve nutrients.
*   **Comparative Nutritional View:** See a side-by-side comparison of the original and optimized recipe's nutritional profiles.
*   **User-Friendly Interface:** A clean and accessible design ensures a seamless and engaging recipe optimization experience.
*   **Scalable Data Integration:** Built to leverage extensive nutritional databases for accurate analysis.

## Tech Stack
*   HTML5
*   CSS3
*   JavaScript (ES6+)
*   External Nutritional Data APIs

## Key Concepts Demonstrated
*   **AI (Artificial Intelligence):** Implementation of intelligent algorithms to process recipe data, understand ingredient properties, and generate context-aware, optimized health suggestions.
*   **Nutritional Analysis:** Deep parsing and evaluation of food items to accurately determine their caloric, macronutrient, micronutrient, and overall health impact.
*   **Recipe Optimization:** Strategic modification of recipes through data-driven ingredient swaps and preparation method changes to enhance health benefits while maintaining flavor.
*   **Data APIs:** Integration with external APIs to fetch comprehensive, up-to-date nutritional information for a vast array of ingredients and food items, forming the backbone of the analysis engine.
