# AI Public Speaking Coach 🚀
> A web application designed to help users refine their public speaking skills. It records user speeches, employs AI to analyze delivery metrics like pace, tone, filler words, and clarity, and provides personalized, actionable feedback with data visualizations.

## Description
The AI Public Speaking Coach is a sophisticated web application designed to significantly enhance users' public speaking abilities. By leveraging advanced artificial intelligence, it records and meticulously analyzes user speeches, providing comprehensive insights into various delivery metrics. This tool aims to empower users with personalized, data-driven feedback to foster continuous improvement in their communication skills.

## Features
*   **Speech Recording**: Easily capture your speeches directly within the browser using the Web Audio API.
*   **AI-Powered Analysis**: Utilizes artificial intelligence to evaluate critical speaking metrics such as pace, tone, clarity, and the presence of filler words.
*   **Personalized Feedback**: Receive actionable recommendations tailored to your specific speaking patterns and areas for improvement.
*   **Data Visualizations**: Understand your performance at a glance with intuitive charts and graphs showcasing your speaking data.
*   **Progress Tracking**: Monitor your speaking evolution over time through historical performance insights.
*   **User-Friendly Interface**: An intuitive and accessible design ensures a smooth experience for all users.
*   **Client-Side Operation**: No complex setup or dependencies required, just open in your browser to start improving.

## Tech Stack
*   HTML5
*   CSS3
*   JavaScript
*   Web Audio API

## Key Concepts Demonstrated
*   **AI**: Application of artificial intelligence algorithms for advanced speech analysis and feedback generation.
*   **Speech Analysis**: In-depth evaluation of vocal delivery metrics including pace, tone, clarity, and detection of filler words.
*   **Public Speaking**: A core focus on helping users improve their skills in delivering effective presentations and speeches.
*   **Web Audio API**: Utilized for capturing, processing, and managing audio input directly within the web browser.
*   **Real-time Feedback**: Providing immediate or near-immediate insights and recommendations post-speech analysis.
*   **Machine Learning**: The underlying technology powering the AI for pattern recognition and predictive analysis in speech.
*   **Data Visualization**: Presenting complex speech metrics in an easily understandable and actionable graphical format.
