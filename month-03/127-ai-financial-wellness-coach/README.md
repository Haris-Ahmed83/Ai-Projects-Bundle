# AI Financial Wellness Coach 🚀
> An intelligent web application that analyzes a user's financial data (transactions, income, expenses) to provide personalized spending insights, proactive budget recommendations

## Description
The AI Financial Wellness Coach is an intuitive web application designed to empower users with a deeper understanding of their financial health. By intelligently analyzing transaction histories, income streams, and expense patterns, it transforms raw financial data into actionable intelligence. This project aims to simplify personal finance management, offering tailored advice and proactive strategies to help users achieve their financial goals and improve their spending habits.

## Features
*   **Personalized Spending Insights**: Get detailed breakdowns and visual representations of your spending habits across various categories.
*   **Proactive Budget Recommendations**: Receive intelligent, AI-driven suggestions for optimizing your budget based on your financial goals and past behavior.
*   **Transaction History Analysis**: Effortlessly track and categorize your financial transactions to understand where your money goes.
*   **Income & Expense Tracking**: Monitor all your income sources and outgoing expenses in one centralized dashboard.
*   **Interactive Financial Visualizations**: Understand complex financial data at a glance through clear and interactive charts and graphs.
*   **Goal-Oriented Financial Planning**: Set and track personal financial goals, such as saving for a down payment or retirement, with intelligent guidance.
*   **User-Friendly Interface**: An intuitive and clean design ensures a seamless and engaging user experience for managing finances.

## Tech Stack
*   HTML5 (Structure)
*   CSS3 (Styling)
*   JavaScript (Client-side logic, data processing, interactivity)

## Key Concepts Demonstrated
*   **AI**: Showcases intelligent data processing to generate personalized insights and proactive recommendations, simulating AI-driven financial advice.
*   **Personal Finance**: Provides tools and features central to managing individual financial health, including tracking, budgeting, and planning.
*   **Data Analysis**: Implements logic to process and interpret user-provided financial data (transactions, income, expenses) to identify patterns and trends.
*   **Budgeting**: Offers functionality for setting spending limits and provides recommendations to help users adhere to and optimize their budgets.
*   **Financial Planning**: Assists users in understanding their financial trajectory and making informed decisions for future financial well-being and goal achievement.
