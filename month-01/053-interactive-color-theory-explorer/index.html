Monochromatic

Variations in lightness and saturation of the same hue.

Analogous

Colors adjacent to each other on the color wheel (e.g., ±30°, ±60°).

Complementary

Colors directly opposite (180°) on the color wheel.

Triadic

Three colors equally spaced (120°) on the color wheel.

Tetradic (Square)

Four colors equally spaced (90°) on the color wheel.
